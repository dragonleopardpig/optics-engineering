Patent Reading Guide
=====================

These records identify useful technical disclosures, their named
inventors, and their publication history. They complement the papers in
:doc:`literature`; a later patent on a particular implementation is not
evidence that its inventors originated the entire technology.

Dates below distinguish the earliest priority reported in the record
from publication of the listed document. Original assignees are reported
where stated. The linked records provide specifications, figures, claims,
family members, and citations; these are starting points for technical
reading, rather than conclusions about present patent rights.


.. _fiber-patent-kawasaki:

P1. Fused multimode access coupler
----------------------------------

**US4291940A — Low loss access coupler for multimode optical fiber
distribution systems.**

* **Inventors:** Brian S. Kawasaki, Kenneth O. Hill, and Derwyn C. Johnson.
* **Original assignee:** Canadian Patents and Development Ltd.
* **Priority / filing:** 13 June 1977. **Published / granted:**
  29 September 1981.
* **Read for:** fused biconical taper geometry, twisting and heating
  methods, and multimode access-coupler fabrication. This gives a detailed
  technical route into the early FBT literature.

`Patent specification and figures <https://patents.google.com/patent/US4291940A/en>`_
and
`original PDF <https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/c5/95/55/082b061ad66f69/US4291940.pdf>`_.


P2. Multiport star distribution
-------------------------------

**US4330170A — Low-loss star couplers for optical fiber systems.**

* **Inventors:** Derwyn C. Johnson, Brian S. Kawasaki, and Kenneth O. Hill.
* **Original assignee:** Canadian Patents and Development Ltd.
* **Priority / filing:** 26 November 1979. **Published / granted:**
  18 May 1982.
* **Read for:** reflection and hybrid transmission/reflection stars made
  from fused, tapered fiber loops. This is a multiport distribution
  architecture, rather than a promise of efficient independent-source
  combining into one SM output.

`Patent specification and figures <https://patents.google.com/patent/US4330170A/en>`_
and
`original PDF <https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/93/24/9c/e47866a8681222/US4330170.pdf>`_.


P3. Wavelength-flattened fused splitting
----------------------------------------

**US4798436A — Optical fused couplers.**

* **Inventor:** David B. Mortimore.
* **Original assignee:** British Telecommunications PLC.
* **Priority:** 30 July 1985. **US filing shown:** 25 July 1986.
  **Published / granted:** 17 January 1989.
* **Read for:** deliberately different propagation constants in the
  coupling region to obtain selected split ratios over a wider wavelength
  range. This addresses broadband power splitting, not merely combining
  distinct wavelength channels.

`Patent specification and figures <https://patents.google.com/patent/US4798436A/en>`_
and
`original PDF <https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/1e/84/5a/3235c056f6c4a0/US4798436.pdf>`_.


P4. Integrated splitter packaging and testing
---------------------------------------------

**EP0907092A1 — Method of manufacturing and testing integrated optical
components.**

* **Inventors:** Patrick Jean Pierre Herve and Thierry Luc Alain Dannoux.
* **Original assignee:** Corning Inc.
* **Priority and filing date reported:** 25 November 1991.
  **This application publication:** 7 April 1999.
* **Read for:** aligning and attaching multiple fiber pigtails before
  separating wafer-level devices, and arranging the pigtails for testing.
  Splitter/combiner structures appear in the disclosure. Its relevance
  is manufacturability and packaging, not an assertion that this is the
  original PLC splitting principle.

`Application specification and figures <https://patents.google.com/patent/EP0907092A1/en>`_
and
`original PDF <https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/1a/f7/9e/b1345fe26310dd/EP0907092A1.pdf>`_.


.. _fiber-patent-digiovanni:

P5. Tapered bundles for cladding-pumped devices
-----------------------------------------------

**US5864644A — Tapered fiber bundles for coupling light into and out of
cladding-pumped fiber devices.**

* **Inventors:** David John DiGiovanni and Andrew John Stentz.
* **Original assignee:** Lucent Technologies Inc.
* **Priority / filing:** 21 July 1997. **Published / granted:**
  26 January 1999.
* **Read for:** bundling and tapering MM input fibers, fusion-splicing
  the bundle to a cladding-pumped fiber, and optionally including an SM
  core for a signal path. Figures 1 and 3 are particularly useful for
  understanding pump-only versus pump/signal combining.

`Patent specification and figures <https://patents.google.com/patent/US5864644A/en>`_
and
`original PDF <https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/a9/94/bb/7cd3fbe58cd27c/US5864644.pdf>`_.


.. _fiber-patent-rothenberg:

P6. Integrated coherent fiber combining
---------------------------------------

**US8184363B2 — All-fiber integrated high power coherent beam combination.**

* **Inventor:** Joshua Elliott Rothenberg.
* **Original assignee listed:** Northrop Grumman Systems Corp.
* **Priority / filing:** 7 August 2009. **Application publication:**
  US20110032602A1, 10 February 2011. **Listed grant publication:**
  22 May 2012.
* **Read for:** phase and polarization control with a tapered-fiber
  combining architecture. This is a useful disclosure for comparing
  coherent field combination with an ordinary incoherent power bundle.

`Granted specification, figures, and PDF download <https://patents.google.com/patent/US8184363B2/en>`_.

How to follow the literature trail
----------------------------------

Read the figures and description to understand the optical paths, then
compare the claims with that description. Follow cited papers backward
for earlier demonstrations and forward citations for later implementations.
Keep one entry per technical disclosure or family in research notes:
application and grant publications are different versions, not independent
experiments. A claimed priority date is also not necessarily a date when
the disclosure became public.
