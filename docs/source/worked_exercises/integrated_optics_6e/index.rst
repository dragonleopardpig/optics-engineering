Integrated Optics — Detailed Worked Solutions
=============================================

This collection accompanies Robert G. Hunsperger, *Integrated Optics:
Theory and Technology*, sixth edition (Springer, 2009),
`DOI 10.1007/b98730 <https://doi.org/10.1007/b98730>`_.
It covers the **208 numbered end-of-chapter problems in all 22 chapters**
of the supplied edition. Problem numbers refer to the book; tasks are
summarized in our own words.

Read :doc:`notation` before starting. Each solution explains the physical
model, develops the necessary equations, substitutes the supplied data where
appropriate, and checks or interprets the answer. Original diagrams show
mode shapes, coupling, modulation, and device geometry. Illustrations are
schematic unless numerical axes are supplied.

Some questions require a value read from a graph, omit a material constant,
or use an approximation outside its range. Those limitations are stated at
the affected problem. A symbolic or conditional answer identifies exactly
which additional datum is needed. Apparent printing errors are distinguished
from explicitly assumed corrections. Historical technology questions are
answered in the context of the 2009 edition.

Numerical models and figures are maintained by
`docs/integrated_optics_calculations.py` and
`docs/generate_integrated_optics_figures.py`. They use SI constants and
explicit conversions; planar-waveguide dimensions in their public functions
are in micrometres. The book PDF is not needed to build these pages.
