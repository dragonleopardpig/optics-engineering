Optics Engineering
==================

A reference for the work in front of you
------------------------------------------

Move between physical principles, engineering estimates, and detailed
derivations. Interactive calculators and browser-based notebooks connect
the mathematics to engineering applications.

.. container:: capability-grid

   .. container:: capability

      **Make an engineering estimate**

      Start with :doc:`knowledge_base/rules_of_thumb` for focal lengths,
      numerical apertures, diffraction limits, and other useful first checks.

   .. container:: capability

      **See the optical system clearly**

      Revisit :doc:`knowledge_base/cardinal_points` and
      :doc:`knowledge_base/lens_design_intro` to connect ray geometry to lens design.

   .. container:: capability

      **Test a relationship interactively**

      Vary an equation in the :doc:`knowledge_base/formula_plotter`, or explore
      detector physics in the :doc:`worked_exercises/photonics_essentials/ch03_interactive_lab`.

   .. container:: capability

      **Build a solution you can check**

      Follow the :doc:`worked_exercises/thin_lens_image_distance` example,
      then use :doc:`worked_exercises/writing_a_solution` to write your own.

.. tip::

   Search by topic or equation with Ctrl/Command+K. Use the appearance controls
   above to choose light, dark, or your system preference.
